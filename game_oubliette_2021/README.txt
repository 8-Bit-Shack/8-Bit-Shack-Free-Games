ABOUT
-----
Oubliette 2021, the sequel: "Adlington's Revenge". The rogue knight Lord
Adlington has trapped the princess Prestbury in his oubliette. Use your chosen
keys to move Sir Shrigley around the maze; every time you land on a pressure
pad a door opens or closes, so plan your route (repeated steps close the doors
again). Rescue the princess in each of the 5 dungeons. The game has a full
MockingBoard soundtrack, a voiced introduction, animation between levels and a
double lo-res ending. Requires an Apple //e (enhanced) with 128K or an Apple
IIgs and a 5.25" drive; the disk has two sides, so you flip it during play. A
MockingBoard is optional. Select uppercase before starting the menu. Keys are
configurable (preset to the arrow keys).

FILES
-----
OUBLIETTE-2021-Disk-A-v1.04.dsk and OUBLIETTE-2021-Disk-B-v1.03.dsk (both DOS
3.3; the two sides of the disk); Oubliette 2021 for the Apple II.pdf (the game
manual); disk label-2.png (disk label banner); source/ (Applesoft BASIC
listings as .bas.txt and Merlin 6502 source as .s, files prefixed disk_a_ or
disk_b_); disk_contents/ (every file from the disk images, named NAME#TTAAAA,
one folder per disk). The graphics, sound and music files are data and have no
source.
